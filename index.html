02 / Comparative cases

Six production cases

Workbook settings, men's tariff lines. Endpoints represent scenarios with 0% and 100% synthetic fibre.

RA values the gap share of the production country at one common rate per minute. Türkiye takes the highest gap share of the register (Bangladesh) as a ceiling default. Jeans and blazer use a 100% synthetic ceiling until composition is declared. These are Task 4.2 conventions. The gap share of Romania stands 0.04 below the share of Bangladesh, and the two rest on different source families.

03 / Evidence and method

Every value has a provenance.

Fee = ((NC + CC + SC) × PHAF − EDB + RSC + RA) × IAF + BWIC.

RA = reference sewing minutes × common rate per minute × gap share of the production country. The gap share is 1 minus (legal minimum wage ÷ living wage).

Capital charge uses the rounded inventory value for an announced plant. BWIC uses the inventory's rounded tier-2 rates and average bale-piece weight. The model uses a proposed tier exemption, distinct from the French simplified declaration threshold.

Workbook scope and integrity rules

TRUSTex T4.2: inventory of references for the simulator, version 5 of 25 November 2026

Scope: three pieces (T-shirt, jeans, blazer) in two production cases (Europe; distant sourcing), calculated with the equation of the reference workbook, version 3: Fee = ((NC + CC + SC) × PHAF − EDB + RSC + RA) × IAF + BWIC.

Change in version 5. RA = reference minutes × common rate per minute × gap share of the production country, where the gap share is 1 minus (legal minimum wage ÷ living wage). The common rate is the living wage per minute of the distant sourcing reference. The gap counted in money (rows RA-07, RA-09, RA-11) stays in the register as context.

Sheet 'Inventory': one row per reference value. Each row carries a unique ID, the term it serves, the piece and the production case, the value, its unit, its status, its source and link, the year of the data, how far the source was read, the date of the check, and how the value is controlled.

Sheet 'Cases': the six calculations. Every value is read from the inventory through its ID, so a change in the inventory updates the cases. Cells in green are read from the inventory; cells in black are formulas.

Sheet 'Gaps': the references that are missing or interim, with the action needed. Sheet 'Risks': the risks of the RA form, with their control.

Sheet 'Sources': the references in Harvard style.

Status vocabulary. MEASURED: official statistic or rate. PUBLISHED: value stated by a named source. CALCULATED: Task 4.2 arithmetic on values of the register. CONVENTION: a decision of T4.2. ILLUSTRATION: a starting value to calibrate. INTERIM REFERENCE: a reference value taken from a secondary or trade source, used until stronger evidence replaces it. DEFAULT (CEILING): no value in hand, so the row takes the ceiling of the reference values of the register (decision of 25 November 2026); rows in green, to replace when a value is found. MISSING: no reference in hand, for context rows that the cases leave unused.

Rules of integrity. One value per row and one unit per value. An ID is never reused. A value changes in the inventory alone. A row without a link states so in its note. A calculated value names the rows it comes from. All amounts in the cases are modelled.
